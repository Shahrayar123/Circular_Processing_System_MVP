BPRD CIRCULAR NO. 44 OF 2026
CONTROLS OVER PRIVILEGED SYSTEM ACCESS

1. All banks shall maintain a register of privileged system accounts and shall review it
on a monthly basis. Evidence of the review shall be retained for inspection.

2. Privileged accounts that remain inactive for ninety days shall be disabled
automatically by the system.

3. Instructions contained in Circular Letter No. 08 of 2021 relating to quarterly review
of privileged accounts shall stand withdrawn.
